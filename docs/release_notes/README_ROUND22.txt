Anna Glass V2 — Round 22 (2026-11-21)

- New bundled wallpapers: "moon" (night lake, default) and "櫻花樹下的水晶茶韻".
- Accent colours work like wallpapers: every folder in images\controls is a
  colour, listed by its folder name. Copy a folder, change its pictures and
  theme.json, and it appears in menu ☰ → Accent colour.
- The card corner ornaments belong to each colour now, so they follow the
  accent (pink, blue, teal, violet, amber, mint).
- All frames follow the accent colour: card outlines, header line, album,
  cover and row frames no longer stay peach or pink in other colours.
- The player buttons, butterfly, volume and corners of every colour are
  remade from the pink set by one rule: no pink glows or rings left. The
  now-playing texts (top right, bottom left) follow the colour as well.
- NATIVE playlist: selected rows use the accent colour; its other colours are
  neutral, so it fits every accent.

Your accent choice carries over ("rose" is now the "pink" folder).
See docs/ROUND22_REVIEW.md. Install this whole skin over the previous build.
