Hariiro — Round 28 (2026-11-28)

- New mini player: menu ☰ → Mini player shrinks the window to a small player at
  the top right (cover, title, artist, playback controls, seek bar, heart). The
  button at its top right, or a double-click on the title, brings the full
  window back. It follows the accent colour and the light / dark interface.
- NATIVE (foobar2000's own list view) was removed; the middle list is always the
  Glass list.
- Fixed: after the mini player the sidebar could stay black until the mouse
  moved over it.
- Fixed: choosing Dark / Light in ☰ → Interface colours now repaints every
  panel at once (some kept the old colours until the next repaint).
