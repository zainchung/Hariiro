Hariiro — Round 17 (2026-11-21)

New: undo for deleting a playlist and clearing the Queue.
- After you delete a playlist in the sidebar or clear the Queue, an Undo button
  shows at the bottom of that panel for 8 seconds.
- The playlist comes back at the same place with the same tracks; the Queue
  gets its old items back (anything queued since stays after them).

See docs/ROUND17_REVIEW.md. Back up playlists-v2.0 and the skin folder, then
install this whole skin over the previous build.
